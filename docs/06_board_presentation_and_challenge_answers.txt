4. CHALLENGE QUESTION ANSWERS

Q1. "How confident are you in these figures?"
Confidence varies by figure, so I grade each one.

High confidence (counted directly, no judgement needed):
- Theatre sessions: 1,538 of 2,174 (70.75%).
- 28 blank HRG codes (GBP 51,800).
- 252 confirmed agency shifts (GBP 97,020).
- 76 RTT breaches among 130 open pathways with a valid wait.

Medium confidence:
- DNA 14.07%.
  - Two non-standard status values affect the third decimal place (14.07% vs 14.10%).
- RTT 41.54%. The export has no referral or clock-start date, so the wait can only be checked against RTT_Weeks.
  - 28 waits were derived from RTT_Weeks.
  - The 312 and 47 long-wait counts quoted previously cannot be reproduced from this extract.
- ED 4-hour compliance is a range, not a point: 79.79% to 83.17%.
  - 39 breach flags are blank and 35 attendances have no departure time.
  - 83.17% assumes all 39 met the target; 79.79% assumes none did.

Low confidence:
- Readmissions read 9.58% from the stored flags but 17.58% when re-derived from dates. 317 inpatient NHS Numbers have conflicting surnames, so neither is reliable yet.
- Bed occupancy cannot be calculated. The extract is a sample of admissions (5% to 15% census), not the full 380-bed hospital.

Q2. "What assumptions did you make?"
Seven assumptions, all documented in the DQ Issue Log and KPI Framework:
1. Theatre cost per session is GBP 4,516.18, back-calculated from the Company Brief's GBP 180,000 a month. No cost per session exists in any export. Finance should replace it.
2. The agency budget is a placeholder: the cost if agency ran at the 8% target. That gives the GBP 50,173.20 variance.
3. The 15 blank agency flags are excluded from the headline. Worst case adds GBP 5,775 (range GBP 97,020 to GBP 102,795).
4. GEN was mapped to GM. This may be wrong for 2 theatre rows and 2 outpatient records that sit in Orthopaedic and Respiratory clinics.
5. RTT is measured on open pathways at 126 days. The negative wait (APT-100200) is excluded.
6. Duplicates: the first record is kept. 2 exact inpatient copies were removed; 4 near-duplicates and 9 ED near-duplicates still need clinical review.
7. ST-99 records are test data and excluded (24 shifts, 4 of them agency).

Q4. "Which KPI would you improve first and why?"
RTT 18-week compliance (KPI 04).
- It is statutory, and NHS England will ask about it in week 1 of the review.
- It is our furthest-from-target KPI: 41.54% vs 92%, a gap of 50.46 points.
- It is the KPI that the other failures feed:
  - DNAs (14.07%) waste slots.
  - Unused theatre sessions (636 a year) are capacity we already pay for.
- Fixing RTT through Orthopaedics, where 17 of 19 open pathways breach, pulls DNA and theatre utilisation up with it.
- The one prerequisite is data. RTT clock-start validation (KPI 18) must start alongside it, so we know which breaches are real before we report the improvement.

Q5. "Why are Finance and Operations calculating theatre utilisation differently?"
They are measuring two different things, and both are arithmetically correct.
- Sessions-based (Finance):
  - Formula: sessions used / sessions available.
  - Answers: did a list run in the slot we funded?
  - Result: 1,538 / 2,174 = 70.75%. This is Finance's 71%.
- Hours-based (Operations):
  - Formula: patient touch time inside the planned session / planned session time.
  - Answers: how much of a list that ran was actually used for surgery?
  - Result: Operations reports 63%.
- What the gap means: the 7.75-point gap between them is time lost inside lists that do run (late starts, early finishes and turnaround), on top of the 636 sessions that never ran.
- Which is the standard: the hours-based measure is the GIRFT national standard and the one benchmarked against peers. The KPI framework makes it the headline (07a), with session uptake shown alongside it (07b).
- Caveat: the 63% cannot be verified from our data, because the theatre system does not export session or anaesthetic times. Different documents have also swapped the labels. The validated export settles it: the sessions-based figure is 70.75%.
- Action: we have asked Daniel to add the four time fields to the theatre system export. Until then, every Board paper names both figures.
