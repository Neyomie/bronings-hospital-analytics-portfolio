3) Theatre capacity. Utilisation is 70.75% on sessions, against 85%: 636 sessions unused a year. At an assumed GBP 4,516.18 per session, that is GBP 239,357.70 a month. Closing the gap to 85% alone is worth GBP 116,630.43 a month.
