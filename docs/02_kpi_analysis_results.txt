4. 30-DAY READMISSION RATE (TASK 19)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Back-fill : The 44 blank Readmission_Flag values were filled with this formula:
            =IF(L2<>"",L2,IF(AND(F2="EMER",COUNTIFS($B:$B,B2,$I:$I,">="&(E2-30),$I:$I,"<="&E2)>0),"Y","N"))
            (Y = emergency admission within 30 days of a previous discharge for the same NHS Number.)
            Result: 10 back-filled as Y, 34 as N.
